Life · Letting go · Meaning

What’s meant for you, will never pass you by

属于你的，不会错过你：关于时机、放手与回望

daphne · elusive nostalgia · Read the original essay ↗ · August 23, 2024

Choose a reading language · 选择阅读语言

In the deepest corner of my being, the belief that what is destined for you will never pass you by has always remained alive, as does the idea that somewhere, beyond what is imaginable to the human mind, the universe arranges things for you with affection.

Perhaps this appears as invisible threads leading us toward certain people or opportunities. For me, it became a truth when confusion engulfed my life: I could not help feeling that something beyond our capabilities or knowledge moves in ways we cannot fully comprehend.

The term hopeless romantic has always carried my name. Since childhood, I have believed there was a reason certain things happened in a certain way, even when no evidence was visible. After bitter experiences, I sent desolate prayers to the sky. Still, I trusted that if something happened beyond my control, perhaps it was destined to be. Who was I to deny it?

“And, when you want something, all the universe conspires in helping you to achieve it.”
— The Alchemist, Paulo Coelho

When things do not unfold as planned, the soul can feel shattered and ask, quietly, why not me? But with time, I began to place small heaps of trust in a larger design unfolding in secret—one I would only understand later. My mother used to tell me, while combing my hair, that there is always something better waiting. Looking back now, I can see how parts of the puzzle made sense.

I have always had the habit of wanting to organise everything: projecting a hypothetical life before my eyes and preparing for every uncertainty life might throw at me. Perhaps it is my Virgo Moon, but I feel safer with a plan. Yet life has a way of demolishing even the most careful plans. So I learned to let go. I stopped gripping people and situations, trying to force them into what I wanted, because what is meant for you does not need to be forced.

Letting go is terrifying, but I have never felt anything so liberating. When you trust, fear begins to dissolve.

“When we are no longer able to change a situation, we are challenged to change ourselves.”
— Man’s Search for Meaning, Viktor E. Frankl

I believe that everything happens for a reason. What you desire may take time, patience and trust, but it will find its way back to you. More than anything, that thought is what keeps me alive.

在我内心最深的角落里，“属于你的，永远不会错过你”这个信念始终活着。还有一种想法：在人类想象力难以抵达的某个地方，宇宙正带着善意为你安排一切。

它也许会以肉眼看不见的线索出现，把你牵向某些人、某些机会。对我而言，当困惑席卷生活时，它逐渐成了一个扎根的事实：我无法不感觉到，在我们能力与知识之外，有某种力量以我们无法完全理解的方式运作。

“无可救药的浪漫主义者”仿佛一直是我的名字。自小时候起，我就相信有些事会以某种方式发生，自有其理由，即使当时生活里看不见证据。经历几次苦涩之后，我曾把绝望的祈祷抛向天空；但我仍相信，如果一件事发生在我的控制之外，也许它就是命中注定。而我又是谁，能去否认它？

“当你真心渴望某样东西时，整个宇宙都会合力帮你实现它。”
—— 保罗·柯艾略，《牧羊少年奇幻之旅》

当事情没有按计划发生，灵魂会像碎成几片，你会轻轻问：为什么不是我？可随着时间过去，我开始把一点一点的信任放进一个想法里：有一个更大的设计正在秘密展开，而我必须在后来才能看见它。母亲曾在给我梳头时告诉我：总会有更好的东西在等着你。现在回头看，我终于能看出那幅拼图怎样逐渐合理。

我一直有一种让人疲惫的习惯：想要安排好一切，先在眼前投射出假想人生，再为生活可能抛来的每一点不确定做好准备。也许是我的处女座月亮，但有计划会让我安心。可是，生活总有办法摧毁最周密的计划。于是我学会了放手。我不再死死抓住人和情境，强迫它们照着我的期待行动，因为真正属于你的，不需要被强求。

放手很可怕，却也是我感受过最自由的事。当你开始相信，恐惧便会一点一点消散。

“当我们无法改变一个处境时，我们就被挑战去改变自己。”
—— 维克多·弗兰克尔，《活出意义来》

我相信一切发生自有原因。你想要的东西或许需要时间，需要耐心，也需要信任；但它终会找到回到你身边的路。比任何事都更重要的是，这个念头让我继续活着。
